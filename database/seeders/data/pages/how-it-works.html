For volunteers

  1. Build your profile. Add your skills, IEEE region and section, and a short bio. The more complete your profile, the better your matches.
  2. Find an opportunity. Search by keyword, skills, category, region, society or duration. Each result shows how well it matches you — and why.
  3. Apply. Tell the organisers why you are interested. You can track every application under My Opportunities.
  4. Log your hours. Once accepted, record the time you contribute. Organisers approve your hours.
  5. Get recognised. When you finish, organisers can rate your contribution and endorse your skills. Everything appears on your profile and in your PDF CV.

For opportunity creators

  1. Create an opportunity using the step-by-step form — or clone one you have run before.
  2. Add co-owners so your team can share reviewing and approvals.
  3. Review applicants with their match score, skills and motivation side by side.
  4. Approve hours and endorse volunteers as the work progresses.
  5. See your impact — volunteers engaged, hours contributed, countries reached and completion rates.
