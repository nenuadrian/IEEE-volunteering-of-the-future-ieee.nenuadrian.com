Who can volunteer?

Any registered user can apply. Some opportunities are limited to particular membership grades — this is shown on the opportunity, and the search page can filter to opportunities matching your grade.

How is the match percentage calculated?

It combines three things: the share of required skills you have (60%), whether your membership grade is eligible (20%), and whether the opportunity is online or in your IEEE region (20%). Hover over the match badge on any opportunity to see the breakdown.

Can I create an opportunity?

Yes — every account can create opportunities and add up to nine co-owners to help manage them.

How do volunteer hours work?

Once you are accepted onto an opportunity you can log hours from My Opportunities. The organisers approve them, and approved hours count towards your impact and CV.

How do I download my volunteer CV?

Open your profile and choose Download PDF CV. You can pick which sections to include and add a personal statement in your profile settings.

Can I hide my profile?

Yes. In your volunteer profile settings you can remove yourself from the public directory and choose whether your email address is shown.
