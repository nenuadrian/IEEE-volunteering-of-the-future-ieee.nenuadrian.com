IEEE Volunteering is the cross-IEEE platform that helps members find meaningful ways to give back — and helps every IEEE unit find the people it needs. Whether you have an hour to spare or want to take on a leadership role, there is an opportunity for you.

Why volunteer with IEEE?

  • Grow your skills. Every opportunity lists the skills it builds — communication, leadership, problem solving, organization and operational acumen.
  • Expand your network. Work alongside members from 160+ countries, across regions, sections, societies and councils.
  • Show your impact. Your hours, completed opportunities and endorsements build a verified volunteer CV you can download and share.

Who can create opportunities?

Every member can. Section and chapter officers, society volunteers, conference organisers and committee chairs all post opportunities here. You can add co-owners to share the work of reviewing applicants and approving hours, and clone a past opportunity to run it again.

Opportunities from volunteer.ieee.org

Opportunities published on volunteer.ieee.org are imported automatically every day, so you can browse everything in one place. If you created one of them, add your IEEE member number to your profile and it will be linked to your account on the next refresh.
